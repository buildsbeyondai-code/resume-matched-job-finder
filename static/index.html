        Unattended auto-apply

        While this app is running, it re-runs the saved searches you switch on, on a schedule, and applies to new jobs that score at or above your minimum, in the same way as Auto apply: the company's own website first, then Naukri, then LinkedIn. It never solves CAPTCHAs, never creates accounts and never guesses answers: anything uncertain waits for you in the daily summary. Nothing runs while the app is closed.

            AI answers

            Sends the form's questions, the job, resume_details.md and your resume text to Claude (Anthropic) through your Claude Code sign-in. Claude answers only from those facts; anything it can't support (salary, notice period, visa, legal consents) is still asked here. Turn off to keep your resume on this computer.

            Job portals (last resort)

            Used only when a company has no application on its own website: Naukri first, then LinkedIn, one application at a time in the app's browser. Automated applying may break these sites' terms of use.

            Portal sign-in
