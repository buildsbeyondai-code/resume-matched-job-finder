# Resume-Matched Job Finder

**Upload your resume, get the jobs that actually fit it, and apply on company sites — with you in control.**

Resume-Matched Job Finder is a local web app that reads your resume, searches several job sources at once, scores
every job against your experience, and can fill in applications on company career sites with answers written by
Claude.

## Features

### Search
- **Resume upload:** your skills, experience and preferences are extracted from your resume.
- **Multi-source search** across Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters and Adzuna, de-duplicated into one list.
- **0–100 match score** for every job, with the reasons behind it.

### Apply — on your terms
- **Application policy modes:** search only (the default), review before applying, or apply automatically within
  rules you set.
- **Auto apply on company sites**, with answers to application questions written by Claude Opus from your resume.
- Job portals are used only as a last resort.
- **Unattended auto-apply** for jobs that pass your policy, with honest results: when a submission can't be
  confirmed it is reported as *outcome unknown*, never as applied.

### Trust
- Runs on your own computer; your resume stays local.
- **Hash-chained audit log** of every search and application, so you can see exactly what was done on your behalf.
